Why It Matters

Local visibility should lead to useful customer actions

A profile is more useful when customers can move naturally from discovery to the next step—visiting the website, requesting directions, calling the business or exploring services.

Discovery

Help searchers understand what the business offers and whether it matches their local intent.

Trust & Clarity

Keep important business information and customer-facing content clear and consistent.

Action

Make the path from Google to the website, directions, calls and other useful customer actions straightforward.

Google Business Profile optimization does not guarantee rankings, calls, visits or sales. Local visibility can depend on relevance, distance, prominence, competition, search demand and Google's ranking systems.
